# CloudPulse Mobile Support

CloudPulse Mobile is a free utility that aggregates cloud service updates from Azure, AWS, Google Cloud Platform, and Microsoft 365 into a single, searchable feed.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Do I need an account to use CloudPulse?**
A: No. CloudPulse is completely free and does not require an account or sign-in.

**Q: How do I enable push notifications?**
A: Go to Settings within the app and toggle on push notifications. You'll be prompted to allow notifications on your device.

**Q: How often are updates refreshed?**
A: Cloud service updates are fetched in real time when you open or refresh the feed. Push notifications for new Azure updates are checked every 15 minutes.

**Q: Which cloud providers are supported?**
A: Microsoft Azure, Microsoft 365, Amazon Web Services (AWS), and Google Cloud Platform (GCP).

**Q: Does CloudPulse collect my personal data?**
A: No. CloudPulse does not collect personal information, use analytics, or serve ads. See our [Privacy Policy](./privacy-policy) for full details.
