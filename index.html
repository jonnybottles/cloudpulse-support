CloudPulse Mobile

Multi-Cloud Update Tracker

Support Privacy Policy

CloudPulse Mobile is a free utility that aggregates cloud service updates from Azure, AWS, Google Cloud Platform, and Microsoft 365 into a single, searchable feed.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an account to use CloudPulse?

No. CloudPulse is completely free and does not require an account or sign-in.

How do I enable push notifications?

Go to Settings within the app and toggle on push notifications. You'll be prompted to allow notifications on your device.

How often are updates refreshed?

Cloud service updates are fetched in real time when you open or refresh the feed. Push notifications for new Azure updates are checked every 15 minutes.

Which cloud providers are supported?

Microsoft Azure, Microsoft 365, Amazon Web Services (AWS), and Google Cloud Platform (GCP).

Does CloudPulse collect my personal data?

No. CloudPulse does not collect personal information, use analytics, or serve ads. See our Privacy Policy for full details.
